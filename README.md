# My Favorite Film Series — The Twilight Saga

![The Twilight Saga: Eclipse](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/700Eclipse.jpg)

## About the Series

**The Twilight Saga** is a fantasy romance film series based on the novels by *Stephenie Meyer*. It follows Bella Swan, a teenager who moves to Forks, Washington, and falls in love with Edward Cullen, a vampire. Her friendship with Jacob Black draws her further into the supernatural world.

---

## Main Characters

| Character | Played by | Role in the Story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bella Swan | Kristen Stewart | A teenager who moves to Forks and falls in love with Edward. |
| Edward Cullen | Robert Pattinson | A vampire who tries to protect Bella while struggling with the dangers of being close to her. |
| Jacob Black | Taylor Lautner | Bella's close friend who discovers he can transform into a wolf. |

## Movies in Watch Order

2. *The Twilight Saga: New Moon* (2009)

4. *The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1* (2011)
5. *The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2* (2012)

## Why I Love It

- The rainy forests and cloudy skies of Forks give the movies a mood I really like.
- I enjoy how Bella and Edward's relationship grows across the five films.
- The rivalry between the vampires and the wolf pack makes the story more exciting.

> "And so the lion fell in love with the lamb."
>
> *– Edward Cullen, Twilight*

My favorite thing about the saga is how it mixes romance with mystery and danger.
